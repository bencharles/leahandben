FAQ

When is the RSVP deadline?
Please RSVP by May 31, 2026. We'd love to hear from you as soon as you're able — it helps us plan the perfect day for everyone. You can RSVP directly from the RSVP page.
What if I need to update my RSVP after submitting?
No problem — you can return to the RSVP page at any time before the deadline to find and update your response. Just be sure to make any changes before May 31, 2026.
Do you have a gift registry?
There will be a card box at the reception for cards and envelopes — we kindly ask that guests not bring boxed gifts. More than anything, having you there with us and keeping us in your prayers means the world to us.
When should I arrive?
The ceremony begins at 12:30 PM at Immaculate Conception Church, and the cocktail hour & reception begin at 5:00 PM at The Tides Estate. We ask that all guests arrive at the church at least 15 minutes early so everyone is seated before the ceremony begins.
How do I get around?
We recommend driving. Parking is available at all three locations — the hotel, the church, and the reception venue — so getting around by car is easy and convenient. See parking details on our Hotel & Travel page. If you prefer not to drive, ride-share services like Uber and Lyft are also an option.
What is the dress code?
We'd love for guests to dress in formal American or Indian attire. All colors are welcome — with the exception of white.

Will the ceremony be indoors or outdoors?
The ceremony will be held indoors at Immaculate Conception Church in Montclair, NJ.
Will there be options for dietary restrictions?
Dinner will be served buffet style, and all dishes will be clearly labeled with ingredients and common allergens.
